# Responsible AI Framework for EcoContradict AI

## 1. Transparency & Explainability
EcoContradict AI does not produce opaque scores or recommendations without actionable rationale. Every finding provides:
- The exact stated goal that triggered the conflict
- The precise planned action detected
- The scientific mechanism explaining why the contradiction exists
- The estimated life-cycle benefit of adopting circular alternatives
- The model confidence level (typically 88%–97%)

## 2. Human-in-the-Loop Governance
Recommendations generated by the platform are decision-support suggestions. Event organizers, municipal planners, and project managers maintain ultimate discretion over final vendor contracts, safety regulations, and budget trade-offs.

## 3. Privacy & Data Minimization
EcoContradict AI parses operational and logistical information (quantities, materials, energy profiles, locations). The system actively filters out sensitive Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as personal credit card details, residential addresses, or medical information.

## 4. Methodological Assumptions
Environmental calculations for waste diversion (kg) and carbon dioxide equivalent reductions (kg CO2e) utilize peer-reviewed standard coefficients from:
- US EPA Waste Reduction Model (WARM v15)
- UK DEFRA Greenhouse Gas Reporting Guidelines
- ISO 14044 Life Cycle Assessment Principles
All metrics are presented with clear advisory notes acknowledging that site-specific variations may apply.
